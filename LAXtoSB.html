LAX to Santa Barbara

Southwest from Greenville through Nashville, landing at LAX Terminal 1 at 1:30 AM and getting to Santa Barbara by morning. Two ways to do it, side by side, with times and what each step costs per person.

Plan A · The Van Nuys hack

FlyAway bus + Amtrak from Van Nuys

$44.75per person
  • Cheapest by a lot
  • Wait at the FlyAway terminal, about 2 hours
  • Backup bus at 3:30 AM if the flight is late
Plan B · Straight to downtown

Uber to Union Station + Amtrak

$56–81per person
  • One ride, no bus or transfer
  • Wait at LAX until 3:30, then about an hour at Union Station
  • Late-night Uber pricing is unpredictable
Same train either way. Pacific Surfliner 757 leaves Union Station at 5:13 AM, stops at Van Nuys at 5:47 AM, and gets to Santa Barbara at 7:56 AM. Both plans arrive at the exact same time; the difference is cost and where you wait.

Plan B

Uber to Union Station

  1. 8:40PM

    Depart GSP

    Southwest WN1953 to Nashville, then WN149 to LAX after a plane change.

  2. 1:30AM

    Land at LAX Terminal 1

    $0

    Stay inside the terminal for now.

  3. 1:30–3:30~2 hr

    Wait in Terminal 1

    Hang out at LAX so we get to Union Station right around 4 AM.

  4. ~3:30AM

    Uber to Union Station

    $25–50

    Walk from Terminal 1 to the LAX-it rideshare lot. About 30 minutes to downtown. Roughly $50–100 for the ride, split two ways.

  5. ~4:00AM

    Arrive Union Station

    $0

    800 N Alameda St. Map ↗

    Why 4 AM: Amtrak only lets people into the station from 1 to 4 AM if their train is in that window, so arriving at 4 means we can walk right in.
  6. 4:00–5:13~1 hr

    Wait at Union Station

    A coffee shop in the concourse opens at 4 AM on weekdays, 5 AM on weekends.

  7. 5:13AM

    Train 757 to Santa Barbara

    $31

    Same train as Plan A, boarding one stop earlier.

  8. 7:56AM

Notes

  • All Uber costs assume we split the ride. The FlyAway fare is $12.75 one way.
  • FlyAway to Union Station isn't an option overnight. The last bus leaves LAX at 1:10 AM and the next at 5:40 AM.
  • Times are from the current posted schedules. Amtrak fares change, so check Amtrak before booking.
