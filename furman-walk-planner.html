Furman campus walking map · interactive mockup

Plan a campus walk

Start anywhere on the walking route. Every walk brings you back to where you began, with the return included in the miles, the minutes and the quarter-mile markers.

Walking pace

Ready-made walks, including the three one-mile segments from the printed map.

Way back

Select stops on the map in the order you want to walk them. If your last stop is not your starting point, the planner adds the shortest way back.

Mockup notes: the route was traced by hand from the printed map and scaled so the full printed route equals 3 miles, so distances are estimates until checked against a GPS measurement. Stop names come from the map's own labels. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
